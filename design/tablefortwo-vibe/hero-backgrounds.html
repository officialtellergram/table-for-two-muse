Glyph Monument

The notched glyph, blown up until it’s architecture: one fine gold arc-pair centred behind the stage, cropped by the frame. The brand mark is the scenery — favicon, nav and hero become the same gesture at three scales. Quietest of the six, most ownable. In production the two arcs can breathe (scale 1 → 1.04 over 12s) without any “spin.”
